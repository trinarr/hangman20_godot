HANGMAN 2.0 Русский

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Hangman 2.0 is made by Nikita Lukanin, an independent game developer and data controller (the “Developer”). The game does not require an account or send your game save to the Developer's servers. For privacy questions, email trinarr@mail.ru.

1. Data on your device

Settings and game progress, including the state of an unfinished game, are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts, cloud synchronization, Developer-operated profile servers, or Developer-operated analytics. The Developer does not receive your local game save.

This data is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

2. Advertising

The game uses Yandex Mobile Ads. Depending on your device, region, settings, and applicable law, the advertising service may process your IP address, advertising identifier and other available device identifiers, approximate location, device and app information, advertising interactions, and technical or diagnostic data.

Yandex may use this information to deliver and measure ads, limit their frequency, prevent fraud and, where permitted by applicable law and your settings, personalize advertising. Yandex processes this information under the Yandex Privacy Policy.

3. Personalized advertising and your choices

Agreeing to the Terms of Service or this Privacy Policy does not by itself constitute consent to personalized advertising or cross-app tracking where applicable law or platform rules require separate consent.

In regions where prior consent is required for personalized advertising, personalized advertising should be used only after that consent has been obtained. In regions where applicable law permits an opt-out model, personalized advertising may be enabled by default, while users must be given an available way to opt out through game, device, or platform settings.

Turning off personalized advertising does not disable advertising altogether: you may still receive contextual or non-personalized ads. Available controls for advertising, advertising identifiers, and tracking depend on your device, operating system, region, and advertising service.

To select the applicable regional advertising-privacy model, the game makes an HTTPS request directly to IPinfo Lite and requests only the two-letter country code of the current public IP connection. As part of the network connection, IPinfo necessarily receives the request IP address; the game does not add your game save, profile identifier, or advertising identifier to this request. IP-based geolocation is approximate and may differ when a VPN, proxy, or roaming connection is used. See the IPinfo Privacy Policy. IP geolocation data is powered by IPinfo.

4. Correspondence and legal pages

If you choose to email the Developer, the Developer receives your sender address, message, and any attachments you provide only to reply, provide support, and investigate bugs.

The legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages without Developer-added forms, analytics, JavaScript, or cookies. When you open them, GitHub may receive standard web-request data such as your IP address and browser information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5. How data is used and disclosed

Local data is used to operate the game and save your progress. Advertising-service data may be used to deliver and measure ads, limit their frequency, personalize ads where permitted, and prevent fraud. Information from emails is used to respond to you, provide support, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

The Developer does not sell personal data for monetary consideration. Data may be disclosed or made available to advertising and technical partners, including Yandex, IPinfo, GitHub, and the email provider, for the purposes described in this Policy, and to public authorities where disclosure is required by law. In some jurisdictions, certain disclosures to advertising partners may fall within special legal definitions of a “sale,” “sharing,” “disclosure,” or “targeted advertising”; where applicable law provides a right to opt out of such processing, users may exercise that right.

6. Legal bases for processing

Depending on the type of data, the purpose of processing, and applicable law, the Developer may process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • to provide the game, handle user requests, and perform obligations arising in connection with your use of the game;
  • the Developer's legitimate interests, such as user support, security, prevention of misuse, dispute resolution, and protection of legal rights, where those interests are not overridden by your rights;
  • compliance with legal obligations and binding requests from public authorities;
  • your consent, where consent is required, including for certain forms of personalized advertising or tracking.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Yandex, IPinfo, GitHub, the email provider, and other independent service providers may determine their own legal bases for processing under their policies and applicable law.

7. Data retention

Local game progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Correspondence with the Developer and related information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to reply and provide support, investigate bugs, resolve disputes, protect legal rights, and comply with legal obligations. After that, such data is deleted or anonymized unless further retention is required by law.

Yandex, IPinfo, GitHub, and the email provider determine their own retention periods under their respective policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of your personal data and obtain access to it;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of data where provided by law;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and, where applicable, targeted advertising;
  • receive data you provided in a portable format where the right to data portability applies;
  • withdraw consent you previously provided;
  • lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

To exercise rights relating to data received directly by the Developer, email trinarr@mail.ru. Where reasonably necessary, the Developer may request additional information to verify the identity of the requester. The Developer has no remote access to your local game save; you can manage it on your device, including by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

9. Children and security

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13 or any higher minimum age required by applicable law. The Developer does not knowingly request personal data from children below the applicable minimum age. Where local law requires permission from a parent or legal guardian, a minor must obtain that permission before using the game.

If you believe a child has provided personal data to the Developer without the required permission, contact trinarr@mail.ru. After receiving sufficient information, the Developer will take reasonable steps to delete such data unless retention is required by law.

The Developer takes reasonable measures to protect received correspondence, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Third-party services may process technical data in other countries under their own policies and applicable law.

10. Changes

This Policy may be updated as the game, third-party services, or legal requirements change. A revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date; the game may display the legal pop-up again for material changes.
