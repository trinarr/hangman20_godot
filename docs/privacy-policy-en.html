Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Hangman 2.0 is made by Nikita Lukanin, an independent game developer and data controller (the “Developer”). The game does not require an account or send your game save to the Developer's servers. For privacy questions, email trinarr@mail.ru.

1. Data on your device

Settings and game progress, including the state of an unfinished game, are stored only on your device. The game has no accounts, cloud synchronization, Developer-operated profile servers, or Developer-operated analytics. The Developer does not receive your local game save.

This data is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

2. Advertising

The game uses Yandex Mobile Ads. Depending on your device, region, settings, and applicable law, the advertising service may process your IP address, advertising identifier and other available device identifiers, approximate location, device and app information, advertising interactions, and technical or diagnostic data.

Yandex uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit their frequency, and prevent fraud. It is processed under the Yandex Privacy Policy.

3. Correspondence and legal pages

If you choose to email the Developer, the Developer receives your sender address, message, and any attachments you provide only to reply, provide support, and investigate bugs.

The legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages without Developer-added forms, analytics, JavaScript, or cookies. When you open them, GitHub may receive standard web-request data such as your IP address and browser information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. How data is used and shared

Local data is used to operate the game and save your progress. Advertising-service data is used to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Information from emails is used to respond to you and meet legal obligations.

The Developer does not sell personal data. Information may be handled by Yandex, GitHub, the email provider, and public authorities where disclosure is required by law.

5. Advertising choices

Agreeing to the Terms of Service is not separate consent to personalized advertising or cross-app tracking. Where consent is required by law, the advertising service should request it separately. Available advertising and advertising-identifier choices can be managed through your device settings.

6. Retention and your rights

Local data remains on your device until it is cleared or the game is uninstalled. Correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to reply and meet legal obligations. Yandex, GitHub, and the email provider set their own retention periods.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of data, restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. For data received by the Developer, email trinarr@mail.ru.

7. Children and security

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13. Where local law requires permission from a parent or legal guardian, a minor must obtain it before using the game.

The Developer takes reasonable measures to protect received correspondence, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Third-party services may process technical data in other countries under their own policies and applicable law.

8. Changes

This Policy may be updated as the game, third-party services, or legal requirements change. A revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date; the game may display the legal pop-up again for material changes.
